[3] Derived conclusions, in the order they changed the contract:
    a) 3 of 3 event types are namespaced and none fits the closed 7-value enum
       -> eventType must be an open union with a reserved core.
    b) actor and source vary independently (4 x 2) -> two dimensions, not one.
    c) status is subject lifecycle, not the outcome of the append -> a separate
       subjectState field, outcome keeps its 5 bounded values.
    d) input_hash 315/315 and file evidence 0/315 -> evidence minItems 1 was
       unachievable for domain events; inputHash carries the binding instead.
    e) receipt_ref and causation_id are "-" on 315/315 -> null, never a
       sentinel; an unexecuted event must not look executed.
    f) mode apply 315/315 -> the PLAN half of the POA split was unobservable;
       mode becomes a required PLAN/APPLY enum.
    g) replayable and kind never vary -> rejected, a constant is not evidence.
    h) no chain at all -> the chain requirement stands and control.jsonl now
       carries two chained events so the rule is exercised, not assumed.
    i) 3 of 6 rotated segments are 0 bytes -> already rejected by
       LOGS-STORE-EMPTY. No change; an existing rule is validated.
